01 Mission file · Provakil · in production

Stop the runaway AI bill

The AI bill hit about five times its normal level and nobody could say which feature was spending it. One gateway now stands between every feature and every model.

Situation

Around 25–30 product features called LLMs directly, each with its own provider integration, and many were bundled into accounts as free trials. There was no way to attribute cost, cap a runaway feature, or keep client personal data from reaching outside providers.

What I built

  • A standalone FastAPI service every feature calls instead of a provider, handling streaming, normal responses and document uploads for Claude, Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek and a self-hosted small model.
  • PII masking with Presidio before any request leaves the network.
  • Wallets and a credit ledger: budgets per team and per developer, separate dev and test budgets, and rate-card chargeback.
  • Its own container, so the same service runs in our cloud and in on-prem client deployments.

02 Mission file · personal project · live

Find the paragraph that wins the case

A research assistant over 11,018 Indian Supreme Court judgments. Ask a legal question or describe your facts, and it answers with citations like Pappu v. Vinod Kumar Lamba, [2018] 1 S.C.R. 195, para 15, linked to that exact paragraph.

Try it live

Clean paragraphs from court reporters

Supreme Court Reports PDFs carry margin letters A–H on every page, editor headnotes, running heads and OCR typos. My ingestion pipeline drops margin letters by their geometry, splits headnotes from the judgment, and follows paragraph numbering through OCR misreads. Across all 11,018 judgments from 2012–2025, numbered-paragraph detection went from 66% to 98.2%, with no failed extractions.

Hybrid search, then cited answers

BGE-M3 dense vectors and BM25 sparse vectors in Qdrant, fused with reciprocal rank fusion. A two-stage citation mode picks judgments using the headnotes, then picks paragraphs inside them. Answers stream from Groq's gpt-oss-120b, falling back to Workers AI Llama 3.3 70B only before the first token, and every [n] citation is checked against the excerpts the model was given.

Case analysis

Describe your facts and an LLM plans the legal issues and search queries. Results from several queries are fused, and a second call writes a research note: similar cases and how they ended for your side, the arguments courts accepted and rejected, and the points the cases don't decide.

Calls the eval made

  • Faithfulness from 0.70 to 93% of answers fully faithful (judged by a second LLM) after adding an attribution rule and passing whole excerpts.
  • Dropped the cross-encoder reranker. On the full corpus it scored no better (MRR 0.741 vs 0.752) and took 4.6 s per query.
  • Query latency from 375 ms to 37 ms by pooling connections to the embedding model.

Production at ₹0 a month

Vercel, Render, Qdrant Cloud, Neon and Upstash free tiers. Redis quotas, a 7-day answer cache and rate limits keep it inside the free model limits. Alembic migrations, JSON logs with request IDs, and GitHub Actions CI with strict mypy, 100 pytest tests and Playwright end-to-end tests.
